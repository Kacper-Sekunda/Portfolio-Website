Diegetic Interactions

The project.

This was a research project where I identified how and why Diegetic Interactions are beneficial towards immersion and engagement. Citing not just academic sources to develop, test and evaluate the system.


What I learnt

This project taught me a lot about procedural animations, and developing reliable testing strategies.


Details

For this project two Solutions were developed, one featuring traditional abstracted interactions and the other featuring 'diagetic' interactions.

The diegetic solution used a combination of procedural animation and input mechanics to stimulate immersion and engagement, mimicking similar inputs to real life, These worked to satisfy a players feedback loop in both visual and physical aspects

It was determined that inclusion of these interactions is benefitial to all player types, highlighting though that they arent so by the virtue of existing. These interactions when given a purpose and implemented well, provide significant improvements to immersion and engagement.
